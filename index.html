How it stays machine-independent

No device names, data-item ids or axis names are coded in this page. Devices come from /probe. A Linear component's position is its POSITION sample. A Rotary component's position is its ANGLE sample. Subtype ACTUAL and the MACHINE coordinate system are preferred. A Rotary component that only reports ROTARY_VELOCITY is treated as a spindle. The motion direction comes from Configuration/Motion/Axis. The axis name is used only if that vector is missing, and the table flags it. The kinematic chain (what rides on what) comes from Motion@parentIdRef and the PARENT/CHILD component relationships. Linear motion is drawn relative to the centre of the observed travel. It is either amplified to fill the model, or shown at true scale against the axis LENGTH specification. The HUD and tables always show the raw values.
